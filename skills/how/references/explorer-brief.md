# Explorer brief

Fill in the question and the angle, and give the rest to the explorer as
written.

---

Other explorers are reading other slices of the same subsystem at the same
time. Stay on your angle and go deep in it. Read only: change no file.

**Question:** {QUESTION}

**Your angle:** {ANGLE}

Report in these sections:

- **Components.** Each type, service, class, or module that matters on this
  angle: its name, its file, and one sentence on what it does.
- **Flow.** The steps in order. For each: the function that runs, its file,
  what it does, what data it passes on, and what it calls next.
- **Files read.** Every file you opened.
- **Boundaries.** Where this slice meets the rest of the system: its inputs
  and its outputs.
- **Surprises.** Anything that works differently from how it looks, depends on
  history, or is easy to get wrong.
- **Open questions.** What you could not trace, and where you stopped.
